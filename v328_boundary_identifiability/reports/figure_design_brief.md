# V328 figure design brief

## Figure 1: boundary-identifiability mechanism

- Scientific question: which unmeasured transformations prevent a reported
  energy value from becoming energy per endpoint-qualified product?
- Takeaway: yield, source conversion, ancillary energy and endpoint alignment
  are identification variables, not decorative reporting details.
- Archetype: physical measurement-boundary mechanism.
- Evidence boundary: equations are bookkeeping identities under declared
  planes; no efficiency or ancillary value is assumed.
- Tool: Matplotlib vector patches for equation-aligned geometry and direct
  SVG/PDF export.

## Figure 2: target-metric comparability graph

- Scientific question: which source-locked records can support one declared
  native-source-input per formed-mass comparison without an unmeasured mapping?
- Takeaway: the 16-record graph has one edge and fourteen isolates.
- Archetype: evidence graph.
- Evidence boundary: audit-set and metric specific; not field prevalence.
- Tool: deterministic route-column layout to keep isolates legible.

## Figure 3: repair complementarity

- Scientific question: does adding the most-missing field alone recover usable
  records, or must fields be supplied jointly?
- Takeaway: mass and energy are complementary; supplying both raises the
  optimistic closure count from two to ten, with a +7-record interaction.
- Archetype: intervention Pareto and feature attribution.
- Evidence boundary: optimistic availability upper bound, not recovered data or
  cross-plane numeric compatibility.
- Tool: Matplotlib scatter/Pareto line plus exact Shapley calculation.

## Figure 4: denominator effect

- Scientific question: how much engineering information is hidden when energy
  is normalized by feed rather than formed product?
- Takeaway: eight JSC-1A cases tie at 18 MJ per feed kilogram but span 18-40.9
  MJ per formed kilogram because yield varies.
- Archetype: paired quantitative result and mechanism curve.
- Evidence boundary: nine source-selected cases from one research lineage; no
  replicate inference or environmental causality.
- Tool: Matplotlib vector data plot.

## Figure 5: sample-observed rank ambiguity

- Scientific question: when can observed yield variation change the ordering
  implied by nominal feed-normalized energy?
- Takeaway: under the correction-factor range observed in nine cases, nominal
  ratios from 0.44 to 2.27 do not fix the formed-product ordering.
- Archetype: set-valued decision consequence.
- Evidence boundary: sensitivity envelope, not a universal yield distribution
  or cross-route performance estimate.
- Tool: analytic Matplotlib band with log axes and explicit limits.
